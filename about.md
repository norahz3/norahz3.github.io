I'm a data scientist on a fundamental research team in commodities. The job is to understand supply, demand, and everything in between well enough to say something useful about what happens next.

I didn't start in data science. Earlier in my career I worked in supply chain and sourcing, and I still think about markets the way I learned to there: as physical things. Ships, harvests, warehouses, and people making decisions with incomplete information.
