For years most of my thinking has lived in places I don't own: work decks, notes apps, conversations that end when the call does. This site is an attempt to keep some of it.

I want a place to write about the things I keep coming back to: how commodity markets actually work, how we think and decide, and what makes something well made.

## What you'll find here

- **Writing**: essays and shorter notes on markets, design, and the mind.
- **Reading**: my library, shelved by topic, with a line or two on each book.
- **Projects**: research and things I've built.
- **Design**: a gallery of things I've made and things I admire.

I'll keep it simple and keep it going. If something here is useful to you, I'd like to hear about it.
