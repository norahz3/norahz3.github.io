A reading note doesn't need to be a review. A useful shape:

1. **The one-sentence argument.** What is the book trying to convince you of?
2. **What held up.** One or two points that matched what you see in your own work.
3. **What didn't.** Where the evidence felt thin, or where the market looks different from inside.
4. **What changed in how I think.** Even a small shift counts.

> Use quote blocks like this for a short line you want to remember.

That's it. Three hundred words is plenty.
